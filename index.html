01 — Screens

Made to be
looked at.

Every screen is one gesture deep: what you came for, in the middle, and nothing asking for attention it hasn’t earned.

02 — Inside

Quietly excellent
where it counts.

Nothing here is a feature for the back of a box. Each one exists because listening to an album on a phone is still, somehow, worse than it should be.

01

Your library, untouched

Read straight from the device. Nothing uploaded, nothing indexed in somebody else’s cloud, nothing to sign into.

02

Gapless, then crossfade

Albums play the way they were recorded. When you want it, tracks melt into each other instead.

03

Lyrics that keep time

Word-level timing, per-singer alignment, and instrumental breaks that breathe instead of sitting still.

04

Colour from the cover

Artwork sets the room. The interface tints itself, then fades back to black so the music stays the bright thing.

05

Eight dimensions, on purpose

A slow orbit of the mix around you. Tuned by ear, adjustable to taste, never a gimmick you can’t turn off.

06

Fast, or it doesn’t count

Cold start to first track in under a second. Animation that runs on the frame, not on a timer.

03 — The player

One screen
you stay on.

  • 01

    Built around the album

    Cover, colour and the next track, arranged so you never have to think about where anything is.

  • 02

    Controls that stay put

    Play stays centred. Everything else moves around it, not the other way round.

  • 03

    Nothing between you and the song

    No feed, no recommendations, no upsell. Open it, press play, put the phone down.
